# Stamina & Strength

A free phone workout app: an 8-week run plan to go from walking to 10 minutes of non-stop running, plus full-body strength workouts with a guided timer, music and a voice coach.

**Open the app:** https://lavanyapro.github.io/stamina-app/

## Install on your phone
- **Android (Chrome):** open the link, tap ⋮ → **Install app**.
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the link, tap Share → **Add to Home Screen**.

Your progress is saved only on your own phone.
